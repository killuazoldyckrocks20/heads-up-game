How to Play Heads Up!

📱

1. Place on Forehead

Pick any deck, tap Play, and hold your phone horizontally against your forehead facing your friends!

🗣️

2. Friends Give Clues

Your friends shout clues, dance, sing, or act out the card on your screen before the 60-second timer runs out!

⬇️

3. Tilt Down = CORRECT!

Got it right? Nod your forehead DOWN towards the floor to score a point and get the next card.

⬆️

4. Tilt Up = PASS!

Stumped? Tilt your forehead UP towards the ceiling to pass on the word with no penalty.

💻

Desktop & Laptop Controls

No phone gyro? Tap the left/right screen zones or use keyboard Down Arrow (Correct) and Up Arrow (Pass)!
